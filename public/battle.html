Battle Map

One hex = 1 inch · tap a token to see who’s in range (Guidebook p. 85) · press and hold the map to ping a spot
MAP SETUP
Nudge grid

Anyone within Arm’s Reach (1″) of a placed Forstall can work it on their turn.

Rough terrain (mud, rubble, deep snow): moving through it costs double Grit (p. 42). Everyone sees it on the map.

Fog of war: the posse sees fog there, and any enemy, NPC or placed Forstall inside it is hidden from them. You see it faintly.

The posse and enemies get their tokens automatically when a fight starts, or when you add enemies.
