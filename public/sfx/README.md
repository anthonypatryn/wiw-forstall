Real sound recordings (free Pixabay sound effects, Pixabay Content License — no attribution needed).
public/js/sound.js plays clips of them (CLIPS: [file, start, length]); a sound with several clips picks one at random.
dice, shuffle (4 shuffles), deal (card snaps), chips/chips2, drink (pour + slam), pistol/pistol2/rifle (gun), shotgun/shotgun2.
Anything without a clip is synthesized. The old way still works: <name>.mp3 listed in manifest.json replaces a sound whole.
